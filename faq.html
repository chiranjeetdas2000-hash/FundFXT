Frequently Asked Questions

Comprehensive information regarding trading rules, payouts, affiliate system, and everything else you need to know about FundFXT.

Direct Funded Model (5K Prototype)

What is the Direct Funded Model?
The Direct Funded Model provides traders with immediate access to a $5,000 virtual account without requiring any evaluation phases. This is a limited prototype. You purchase the account, trade following the rules, and can withdraw profits after the trading cycle.
How much is the entry fee for Direct Funded?
The standard entry fee is $10. However, if you use a valid affiliate coupon code, the price may be reduced (up to $5 off). The final price is determined by the coupon applied.
What are the trading rules for the Direct Funded account?
To keep the account active and eligible for payout, you must adhere to the following rules:
  • Account Size: $5,000
  • Daily Drawdown: 2% ($100) based on balance
  • Max Floating Drawdown: 5% ($250)
  • Max Trades per Day: 3
  • Max Lot Size: 1.00
  • Minimum Trading Days: 10
  • News Trading: Not Allowed
  • Max Open Position: 1 at a time
  • Consistency Score: 35%
When can I withdraw my profits?
The trading period lasts 30 days. Once the 30-day cycle ends, you can request a payout within 7 days if you remain eligible. The maximum withdrawal is $100, and it is allowed only once per account. After this payout, the account is closed.
What happens after I receive my payout?
The account is permanently closed after the single payout. Future prototype models may have different caps and rules, adjusted based on liquidity. New accounts can be purchased for new cycles.

2-Step Challenge Model

What is the 2-Step Challenge?
The 2-Step Challenge is a funded account program designed for disciplined traders. You must complete two evaluation phases to earn a funded account.
What are the challenge phases?
  • Step 1: Profit Target $400
  • Step 2: Profit Target $250
How much is the entry fee for the 2-Step Challenge?
The standard entry fee is $29. If you use a valid affiliate coupon code, the price may be reduced (up to $20). The final price is determined by the coupon applied.
What are the rules for both challenge phases?
  • Account Size: $5,000
  • Daily Drawdown: $250 based on balance
  • Max Drawdown: $400 based on balance
  • Max Lot Size: 1.00
  • Minimum Trading Days: 5 per step
  • News Trading: Not Allowed
  • Max Open Position: 1 at a time
  • Consistency Score: 45% (Challenge Phase)
When can I withdraw after completing the challenge?
Payouts are allowed 7 days after the first trade on your funded account. The withdrawal limit scales with performance: $200 (first 3), $500 (next 3), and $1000 (subsequent).

Payouts & Withdrawals

What is the minimum payout amount?
There is no minimum payout threshold for traders. However, for affiliates, the minimum payout is $100.
How are payouts processed?
Payouts are processed manually within 2-3 business days after your request. Funds can be transferred via UPI, Crypto, or Bank Transfer, depending on your selected method.
Why is my account closed after the Direct Funded payout?
The Direct Funded prototype is designed as a single-cycle test. The account closes after the one-time payout to manage risk and liquidity. Future models will have different caps and cycle structures.

General & Platform

Is FundFXT a real broker?
No. FundFXT is a simulated trading environment. We provide virtual capital to traders and manage payouts from our internal reserve pool. We do not execute trades on real markets.
Can I trade during news events?
No. News trading is strictly prohibited for both the Direct Funded and 2-Step Challenge models.
What happens if I breach a rule?
If you breach any trading rule (such as exceeding the max drawdown or daily loss), your account will be permanently closed and you will not be eligible for any payout.
Can I have multiple accounts?
No. Each user is limited to one active account per model (Direct Funded or 2-Step Challenge). This ensures fair trading and prevents abuse.
