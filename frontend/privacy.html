Privacy Policy

How FundFXT collects, uses, shares, protects, and retains information when you use its simulated trading platform.

Simulated trading: FundFXT provides a simulated trading environment. Orders are not sent to real markets, and simulated trading activity does not represent actual market execution or real-world trading results.
01 · Introduction

Introduction

FundFXT operates a simulated trading platform. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected when you register for, access, or use the platform and how that information may be used.

FundFXT is currently in its testing phase. Data practices may be refined as the platform is tested and developed.

By using the FundFXT platform, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy and the information practices described here.

02 · Collection

Information We Collect

2.1 Registration Data

  • Legal name: Information provided when registering or managing an account.
  • Email address: Used for account access, verification, support, and platform communications.
  • Phone number: Collected where requested for account or platform operations.
  • Password: Account passwords are stored in protected form rather than as plain-text passwords.

2.2 Account & Trading Activity

  • Simulated trade records: Information such as orders, entries, exits, volumes, timestamps, and calculated trading results.
  • Performance metrics: Information used to evaluate simulated account performance.
  • Consistency scores: Metrics used where applicable under platform rules.
  • Drawdown usage: Account risk and drawdown information used to apply applicable trading rules.

2.3 Technical Data

  • IP address: May be collected for security, abuse prevention, and platform operations.
  • Browser information: Browser type and related technical characteristics.
  • Device information: Technical information about the device or environment used to access the platform.
  • Usage patterns: Information about how the platform is accessed and used to improve reliability and functionality.
03 · Use

How We Use Information

Information collected through the platform may be used for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the FundFXT platform.
  • To process purchases, account-related transactions, payouts, and withdrawals.
  • To determine account and payout eligibility under applicable platform rules.
  • To communicate with users about accounts, transactions, support requests, and platform matters.
  • To improve platform security, reliability, performance, and user experience.
  • To comply with applicable legal or other requirements that apply to the platform or a particular request.
04 · Sharing

Data Sharing

FundFXT does not sell personal information. Information may be shared in limited circumstances necessary to operate the platform or address applicable requirements.

  • Service providers: Limited information may be shared with providers that support payments, hosting, infrastructure, communications, or other platform operations.
  • Legal requirements: Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law or a valid legal process.
  • Affiliate tracking: Limited information may be used or shared for referral verification, attribution, and commission processing.

Data minimization: Sharing is intended to be limited to information reasonably needed for the relevant service, verification process, or requirement.

05 · Security

Data Security

FundFXT uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or loss.

  • Passwords are stored in protected form rather than plain text.
  • Access controls are used to restrict access to information and platform systems where appropriate.
  • Security practices may be reviewed and refined as the platform develops.

Users are also responsible for protecting their account credentials, using appropriate device security, and promptly reporting suspected unauthorized account access to support.

06 · Storage

Cookies & Storage

  • Cookies may be used to support sessions, preferences, and platform functionality.
  • Local storage may be used to retain user preferences or session-related information in the browser.
  • Users can control or restrict cookies through their browser settings.
  • Some platform features may not function correctly if required cookies or browser storage are disabled.
07 · Retention

Data Retention

FundFXT keeps information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and for related platform operations.

  • Account data: Generally retained while an account is active and for as long as reasonably necessary for account-related operations.
  • Transaction records: Retained as needed for support, dispute resolution, transaction history, and platform operations.
  • Retention periods may vary based on the nature, purpose, and operational need associated with the information.

When information is no longer needed for its relevant purpose, it may be deleted, anonymized, or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable requirements and operational practices.

08 · Rights

User Rights

Users may contact FundFXT support to request information about their personal data and, where applicable, request:

  • Access: A copy or description of personal information held about them.
  • Correction: Correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Deletion: Deletion of personal information, subject to applicable requirements and legitimate operational needs.

Requests can be submitted to support.fundfxt@gmail.com. Additional information may be requested to verify the account or identity associated with a request.

09 · Updates

Changes to This Policy

FundFXT may update this Privacy Policy as the platform, its features, or its data practices develop.

  • Updated versions will be posted on this page.
  • The published page should be reviewed periodically for changes.
  • Continued use of the platform after an update indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
10 · Contact

Contact FundFXT

For questions about this Privacy Policy or requests concerning your personal information, contact FundFXT support.
