Authoritative Rules

Payout Rules

How eligible simulated trading profits move from a funded account to the FundFXT Wallet and then to an external withdrawal method.

Simulated trading: All trading on FundFXT is simulated. Orders are not sent to real markets. Payout rules apply to the platform's simulated account and wallet flows.
01 · Two-phase model

Two-Phase Payout Model

FundFXT separates the payout process into two stages: an eligible funded-account profit transfer into the FundFXT Wallet, followed by a wallet withdrawal request to an external method.

PhaseFlowStatus
Phase 1 (B.3)Funded Account → FundFXT WalletOn account approval
Phase 2 (B.2)FundFXT Wallet → ExternalOn wallet request
02 · Account payout

Phase 1: Account Payout

Warrior withdrawal tiers

Warrior funded accounts use a tiered maximum amount based on the number of approved account withdrawals.

Account payout requirements

  • No open trades at the time of the request.
  • All applicable account rules must be met.
  • At least $100 of realized profit is required for the funded-account transfer.
  • A 7-day cooldown applies per account from the last approved account payout.
03 · External withdrawal

Phase 2: External Withdrawal

After eligible funds are available in the FundFXT Wallet, a user can request an external withdrawal through an available method.

Wallet rule: A 14-day cooldown applies from the last APPROVED wallet withdrawal, and only one pending wallet withdrawal may exist at a time.

Minimum withdrawal amounts

MethodMinimum
UPI$10
Crypto$25
Bank$100

Manual review

External withdrawal requests are subject to manual admin review. The review may verify the request details, account history, eligibility, and applicable platform rules before a decision is recorded.

04 · Fixed liquidity

Fixed Liquidity Disclosure

FundFXT operates with fixed liquidity. If total approved payouts exceed available liquidity, distributions will be made on a pro-rata basis across all eligible traders.

Payout availability is therefore subject to available liquidity and the operational conditions of the platform.

05 · Processing

Payout Processing

  • Processing is handled on a best-effort basis.
  • No specific payout processing timeline is stated.
  • All payout activity is subject to available liquidity.
  • Manual admin review applies to all payout and withdrawal requests.

Review principle: Eligibility is assessed against the applicable account, wallet, and payout rules in effect for the platform.
