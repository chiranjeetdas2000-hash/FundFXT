Authoritative Rules

Affiliate Rules

Current rules for referral commissions, affiliate wallet earnings, and affiliate transfers within the FundFXT simulated platform.

Pre-launch notice: FundFXT is currently in pre-launch phase. This is a simulated trading environment. Services, rules, and offerings may change as we refine our platform.
Simulated trading: All trading on FundFXT is simulated. Orders are not sent to real markets. Affiliate commissions relate to verified platform purchases and wallet flows.
01 · Overview

Affiliate Overview

  • All registered users can participate as affiliates.
  • No minimum sales threshold is required to start.
  • Commission is credited after the referred purchase is verified.
  • Affiliate wallet earnings accumulate and transfers follow the applicable platform rules.
02 · Commission

Commission Formula

The commission is calculated using the final amount actually paid for a verified referred purchase:

Commission = floor(final_amount_cents × 20% + 100 cents)

03 · Verification

Verification & Crediting

  • Commissions are credited only after the referred payment is verified.
  • Duplicate detection prevents double-counting of the same order.
  • Self-referrals are not allowed.

Commission records are associated with the verified referral transaction so that a single purchase is not counted more than once.

04 · Self-referral

Self-Referral Prevention

Self-referrals are strictly prohibited. Any attempt to refer your own purchases may result in reversal of commissions and account review.

05 · Wallet & transfers

Affiliate Wallet & Transfers

  • Verified commissions accumulate in the affiliate wallet.
  • Transfer to the FundFXT Wallet is available through admin-approved requests.
  • Transfer mode may be set to Manual or Automatic by admins.
  • Transfers are subject to review and the applicable platform rules.

Wallet principle: The affiliate wallet records accumulated commission earnings separately from external withdrawal processing.

06 · Processing

Payout Processing

  • Affiliate payout activity is subject to available liquidity.
  • Processing is handled on a best-effort basis.
  • No specific payout processing timeline is stated.
  • Manual admin review applies to affiliate payout and transfer requests.
