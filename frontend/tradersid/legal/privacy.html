Privacy Policy

How Traders ID collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

Last updated: 5 October 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FundFXT, the operator of Traders ID, collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data when you use the Traders ID platform. This Policy is governed by the laws of India and complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of data:

  • Account data: name, email, password (hashed), Trader ID, profile details (bio, country, social links, avatar).
  • Verification data (KYC): government-issued ID, address proof, selfie, country of issuance, and related documents you upload.
  • Trading data: statements, account snapshots, and performance metrics you choose to upload or authorize.
  • Technical data: IP address, device fingerprint, browser/OS, timezone, and access logs.
  • Payment data: order references and payment status (we do not store full card numbers; payments are processed by third-party gateways).
  • Communication data: support emails and messages you send to us.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data to:

  • Create and manage your account and Trader ID.
  • Verify your identity and issue verification badges.
  • Display your public profile card to viewers who hold your access key.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, duplicate accounts, and unauthorized access.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.
  • Improve the Service and communicate service updates.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use essential cookies and browser local storage to keep you logged in, remember preferences, and secure your session. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can clear cookies/local storage from your browser settings; this will log you out and may affect functionality.

5. Third Parties & Sub-processors

We share data only with trusted sub-processors needed to run the Service:

  • Cloudflare R2 — encrypted file storage (statements, KYC docs, avatars).
  • Render — backend hosting.
  • Aiven MySQL — encrypted database hosting.
  • Vercel — frontend hosting and CDN.
  • Email delivery provider — for verification and transactional emails.
  • Payment gateways — for processing payments when applicable.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active and for a limited period afterwards as required by law (typically up to 3 years for KYC and financial records). You may request deletion at any time, subject to legal retention obligations.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing.
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, email us at support.fundfxt@gmail.com.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures including AES-256 encryption for sensitive files, TLS for data in transit, access controls, and encrypted credentials. No system is perfectly secure; you are responsible for keeping your password and access keys confidential.

9. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we learn that a minor has created an account, we will delete it promptly.

10. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside India where our sub-processors operate (e.g., Cloudflare, Render, Vercel). We take reasonable steps to ensure such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified through the Service or by email. Continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests:

Email: support.fundfxt@gmail.com
Location: India
