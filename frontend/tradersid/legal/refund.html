Refund Policy

Our policy on refunds and cancellations for paid Traders ID products.

Last updated: 5 October 2026

On this page

Digital Products Physical Cards Order Cancellation Failed Payments Disputes Chargebacks Contact

1. Digital Products — Non-Refundable

All digital products are non-refundable once delivered. This includes verified badges, digital card activation, profile features, and any other instantly-issued digital item.

Because digital items are made available to you immediately upon successful payment and cannot be "returned", we do not offer refunds for them once they have been issued — except where required by applicable law.

2. Physical Cards

Physical Trader ID cards are custom-produced and shipped to your address. Once an order has been dispatched, it is non-refundable. Before dispatch, you may request cancellation (see Section 3).

If a physical card arrives damaged or incorrect, contact us within 7 days of delivery with photographic evidence, and we will arrange a replacement at our discretion.

3. Order Cancellation

  • Digital products: Cannot be cancelled once issued.
  • Physical cards: Can be cancelled only before the order enters production/dispatch. Contact us as early as possible.

4. Failed Payments

If your payment fails but an amount is debited from your account, it is typically reversed automatically by your bank or payment provider within 5–10 business days. If not, contact us with your transaction reference and we will assist.

5. Disputes

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, contact us first at support.fundfxt@gmail.com within 7 days of the charge with your order reference. We will investigate and respond within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Chargebacks

Initiating a chargeback without first contacting us may result in suspension of your account and Traders ID. We reserve the right to contest chargebacks that we believe are unjustified, and to share relevant transaction records with the payment provider as required.

7. Contact

For refund and billing questions:

Email: support.fundfxt@gmail.com
Location: India
