About FundFXT

About FundFXT

A simulated prop firm environment built to test trading models, account rules, and payout workflows on the platform.

Simulated trading: All trading on FundFXT is simulated. Orders are not sent to real markets, and simulated performance does not represent actual market execution or real-world trading results.
01 · Platform

What is FundFXT?

FundFXT is a simulated prop firm environment where traders can purchase evaluation accounts and demonstrate trading skill under defined platform rules.

  • Traders purchase evaluation accounts to demonstrate their trading skills.
  • Successful traders may become eligible for payouts under the applicable rules.
  • All trading activity is simulated.
  • No trades are executed in real financial markets through the platform.

03 · Process

How It Works

  1. Purchase an applicable challenge or account offering.
  2. Trade under the platform's simulated market conditions.
  3. Meet the applicable account and risk rules.
  4. Eligible traders can request account payouts and wallet withdrawals under the current payout and withdrawal rules.

Current rules: Eligibility and account outcomes are determined by the applicable rules published on the platform.

04 · Roadmap

Growth Roadmap

FundFXT is using its testing phase to test the business model and refine the platform before broader expansion.

  • A dedicated domain may be introduced in the future.
  • Additional account sizes and features are planned and may be added as the platform develops.
  • Business model testing is part of the current testing phase.
  • Further refinements may be made based on user feedback and platform testing.

Transparency note: Planned items are subject to change and should not be treated as a commitment to a particular feature, offering, or launch schedule.

05 · Responsibility

Responsible Trading

  • FundFXT provides a simulated trading environment only.
  • No financial advice is provided through the platform.
  • Users are responsible for their own trading decisions and use of the platform.
  • Simulated performance does not predict real-world trading results.
06 · Liquidity

Fixed Liquidity Context

FundFXT operates with fixed liquidity. Payout activity is therefore subject to the liquidity available to the platform at the relevant time.

Liquidity disclosure: If approved payouts exceed available funds, distributions will be made on a pro-rata basis across all eligible traders.
