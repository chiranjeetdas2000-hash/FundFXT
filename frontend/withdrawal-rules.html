Authoritative Rules

Withdrawal Rules

Current rules for moving eligible funds from a funded account to the FundFXT Wallet and from the wallet to an external withdrawal method.

Pre-launch notice: FundFXT is currently in pre-launch phase. This is a simulated trading environment. Services, rules, and offerings may change as we refine our platform.
Simulated trading: All trading on FundFXT is simulated. Orders are not sent to real markets. Withdrawal rules apply to the platform's simulated account and wallet flows.
01 · Overview

Withdrawal Overview

FundFXT uses two separate paths for eligible profit movement:

PathPhaseFlow
Account PayoutPhase 1 (B.3)Funded Account → FundFXT Wallet
External WithdrawalPhase 2 (B.2)FundFXT Wallet → External Method

These paths are reviewed separately. An account payout makes eligible funds available in the FundFXT Wallet; an external withdrawal is a request to move available wallet funds through a supported method.

03 · Cooldowns

Cooldown Rules

LevelRule
Account-level
Phase 1		7 days per account from the last approved account payout.
Wallet-level
Phase 2		14 days from the last APPROVED external withdrawal.

Cooldown reminder: A new request must satisfy the applicable cooldown before it can be reviewed.

04 · Pending limit

One-Pending-At-A-Time Rule

Only one pending external withdrawal may exist at a time.

This rule helps prevent duplicate or overlapping requests and keeps the review state clear for each withdrawal.

05 · Rejection

Rejection Handling

If a withdrawal request is rejected:

  • The request does not become an approved external withdrawal.
  • The wallet balance remains as determined by the platform's recorded state.
  • A reason may be provided by the reviewing admin.
  • The user can submit a new request subject to the applicable cooldown and withdrawal rules.
06 · Processing

Processing & Review

  • Manual admin review applies to all external withdrawal requests.
  • Processing is handled on a best-effort basis.
  • No specific withdrawal processing timeline is stated.
  • All withdrawal activity is subject to available liquidity.

Liquidity disclosure: FundFXT operates with fixed liquidity. If total approved payouts exceed available liquidity, distributions will be made on a pro-rata basis across all eligible traders.
