Refund Policy

Refund Policy

Guidelines for refund requests relating to challenge and account purchases on the FundFXT platform.

Pre-launch notice: FundFXT is currently in pre-launch testing phase. Policies, offerings, and platform workflows may evolve as the platform is refined through testing and user feedback.
Simulated trading: FundFXT provides a simulated trading environment. Orders are not sent to real markets, and simulated performance does not represent actual market execution or real-world trading results.
01 · Overview

Refund Policy Overview

This policy describes how refund requests for challenge and account purchases may be reviewed on the FundFXT platform.

  • This policy applies to purchases made through the FundFXT platform.
  • Refund requests are subject to review based on the circumstances of the request.
  • Supporting information may be requested where necessary to verify a payment or platform issue.
  • Refund processing is handled on a best-effort basis, and no guaranteed refund timeline is provided.
02 · Eligibility

When Refunds May Apply

A refund request may be considered in circumstances such as:

  • A duplicate charge or double payment for the same purchase.
  • A verified technical error that prevents access to the purchased account.
  • Payment being processed while the account is not created because of a verified platform error.
  • Other circumstances that FundFXT may consider at its reasonable discretion after reviewing the available information.

Review basis: Each request is assessed based on the payment record, account state, technical evidence, and circumstances provided.

03 · Exclusions

When Refunds Do Not Apply

Refund requests generally do not apply where the purchase has been used in circumstances including:

  • Rule violations, including drawdown breaches or applicable consistency failures.
  • Account closure resulting from prohibited conduct or other applicable account violations.
  • A change of mind after access to the purchased account has been granted.
  • Completed trading activity on the account.
04 · Request

How to Request a Refund

  1. Contact FundFXT support at support.fundfxt@gmail.com.
  2. Include your account email, order ID if available, and a clear description of the issue.
  3. Await review and a response from support.
  4. If approved, the refund is processed on a best-effort basis according to the applicable payment and platform conditions.
support.fundfxt@gmail.com
05 · Processing

Refund Processing

  • All refund requests are subject to manual review.
  • Processing is handled on a best-effort basis.
  • No specific refund processing timeline is stated.
  • Where a refund is approved, the refund method depends on the original payment method and applicable payment-provider conditions.
  • Refund processing is subject to available liquidity where applicable.

Processing note: A request being submitted does not by itself mean that a refund has been approved. The outcome depends on the review of the specific circumstances.

06 · Liquidity

Fixed Liquidity Notice

Liquidity disclosure: FundFXT operates with fixed liquidity. Refund processing is subject to available resources at the time of the request.

07 · FAQ
