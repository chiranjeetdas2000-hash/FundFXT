05 · Risk mechanics

How Drawdown Is Measured

Warrior — trailing balance HWM

The Warrior maximum drawdown uses the account's trailing balance high-water mark. As the relevant account balance high-water mark increases, the drawdown threshold trails that high-water mark.

Prototype — trailing equity HWM

The Prototype maximum drawdown uses a trailing equity high-water mark. The applicable threshold therefore follows the highest recorded equity level under the model's risk calculation.

Risk reminder: Daily drawdown is measured from the opening balance of the day. Maximum drawdown and daily drawdown are separate limits.

06 · Breaches

Breach Consequences

If an account reaches an applicable drawdown or other rule boundary, the account may be settled and closed according to the applicable model and platform state.

  • Open trading activity is stopped when an account is breached.
  • The account status is updated according to the applicable breach outcome.
  • Any payout eligibility is determined separately under the current payout rules.
07 · FAQ

Common Questions

Hitting the applicable maximum drawdown boundary is treated as a breach. Trading is stopped and the account is settled and closed according to the applicable model rules.

FundFXT uses a simulated trading environment. There is no separate news-trading prohibition stated in the current challenge rules. Any future platform restriction will be communicated through the applicable rules.

Consistency limits how much the highest profitable trading day can contribute to overall profit. For Warrior the limit is 55%; for Prototype it is 30%.
