Payout Process & Methods

Learn how FundFXT processes payouts for traders and affiliates. This page details the accepted methods, timelines, and rules you need to know.

Available Payout Methods

FundFXT offers multiple secure payout methods to accommodate traders worldwide. Below are the accepted channels for receiving your earnings:

Crypto (USDT/BTC)

Fastest and most universal method. Payouts are sent within 24 hours to your provided wallet address.

Bank Transfer

Direct transfer to your local bank account. Processing time: 2-3 business days.

UPI (India)

Instant and reliable for Indian traders. Transfers are completed within 24 hours.

How Payouts Work

The payout process is designed to be transparent and straightforward. Follow these steps to receive your earnings:

1

Complete Trading Cycle

For Direct Funded accounts, complete the 30-day trading period. For 2-Step Challenge, complete the funded phase.

2

Request Payout

Navigate to your Dashboard, select your account, and submit a payout request.

3

Verification

Our team verifies your trading performance, consistency, and rule compliance within 24-48 hours.

4

Receive Funds

Funds are transferred using your preferred method. Crypto: 24 hours, Bank/UPI: 2-3 business days.

Trader Payout Rules

Payout limits and eligibility depend on the account model you choose:

  • Direct Funded Model: Maximum withdrawal of $100 (one-time). The account closes after this payout.
  • 2-Step Challenge Model: Scaling withdrawal limits:
    • 1st–3rd Withdrawal: $200 per payout
    • 4th–6th Withdrawal: $500 per payout
    • 7th+ Withdrawal: $1000 per payout
  • Eligibility: Payouts are available 7 days after your trading cycle is completed.

Affiliate Payout Rules

Affiliates earn commissions on every verified sale. The payout process for affiliates is as follows:

  • Commission: $2 per Direct Funded sale, $5 per 2-Step Challenge sale.
  • Minimum Payout: $100 (You must accumulate at least $100 in commissions before requesting a payout).
  • Payout Methods: Crypto, Bank Transfer, or UPI.
  • Processing Time: 2-3 business days after request submission.

Important Notes & Security

  • One Account, One Payout (Direct Funded): Each Direct Funded account is eligible for exactly one payout. After the payout, the account is permanently closed.
  • Manual Verification: All payouts are manually verified to ensure compliance with trading rules and prevent fraud.
  • No Real Funds Trading: FundFXT operates in a simulated environment. Payouts are issued from our internal reserve pool.
  • No Payout for Rule Breaches: If any trading rule (daily drawdown, max drawdown, news trading, max lot, etc.) is breached, the account is closed and no payout is issued.
  • Future Models: Payout caps may change in future prototype models based on liquidity, but the core process remains the same.
